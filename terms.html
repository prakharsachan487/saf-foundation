Terms of Service & Giving Guidelines

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • S. A. Foundation (SAF)

Welcome to the official portal of S. A. Foundation (SAF). By using this website, making a charitable donation, or enrolling in volunteer programs, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and operational policies.

1. Voluntary Charitable Contributions

All donations made to S. A. Foundation are voluntary and intended solely for non-profit charitable activities, including rural smart tablet education, disaster flood relief, solar water installations, and women welfare.

2. Official Donation Receipts & Compliance

All donations made to S. A. Foundation receive an automated digital donation receipt with a unique verification ID for donor accounting records. Donors are requested to provide accurate contact information to ensure seamless receipt delivery.

3. Donation Cancellation & Refund Policy

Since contributions are deployed rapidly into ground humanitarian emergencies and procurement of student learning kits, donations are ordinarily non-refundable. However, in cases of inadvertent double payment or technical banking duplication, a refund request can be filed within 7 days of transaction with payment transaction proofs sent to contact@saf-foundation.org.

4. Intellectual Property & Dispatches

All photographs, dispatches, 3D interactive graphics, and brand materials published on this website are the intellectual property of S. A. Foundation and protected under Indian copyright law.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any disputes arising shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in New Delhi, India.
