Governance & Stewardship

Board of Trustees & Leadership

Guiding S. A. Foundation with unwavering fiduciary responsibility, executive rigor, and deep grassroots empathy to transform millions of underserved lives across India.

Board of Trustees

Anand Singh

Trustee & Managing Trustee, S. A. Foundation
“Social responsibility of each individual builds and economically empowers resilient communities.”
— Anand Singh, Managing Trustee

Anand Singh channeled his entrepreneurial success and social vision into building scalable grassroots systems across India. Under his dedicated stewardship, S. A. Foundation has mobilized hundreds of millions in relief aid, established modern digital classrooms, and fostered self-reliant village cooperatives.

His hands-on ground leadership ensures every rupee contributed directly reaches the vulnerable child, mother, and farmer, fostering generational socio-economic resilience.

Board of Trustees

Rashmi Singh

Trustee & Executive Director, S. A. Foundation
“Empowering women and educating children creates an unstoppable ripple effect of generational prosperity.”
— Rashmi Singh, Trustee

Rashmi Singh spearheads the Foundation's women welfare initiatives, maternal health programs, and rural girl-child STEM scholarships. With over a decade of hands-on community mobilization experience, she drives grassroots micro-entrepreneurship and solar handicraft clusters.

Her strategic focus on community ownership ensures sustainable health, nutrition, and education models that empower rural women as self-reliant decision makers.
