Leadership & Vision — Anand Singh

Visionary leadership fusing corporate execution rigor with deep grassroots empathy to transform millions of lives across India.

Anand Singh

Founder & Managing Trustee, S. A. Foundation
“Social responsibility of each individual builds and economically empowers communities.”
— Anand Singh

Anand Singh channeled his entrepreneurial success into building scalable grassroots systems across India. Under his visionary stewardship, S. A. Foundation has mobilized hundreds of millions in relief aid, established modern digital classrooms, and fostered self-reliant village cooperatives.

His hands-on ground leadership ensures every rupee donated directly reaches the vulnerable child, mother, and farmer, fostering generational socio-economic resilience.
