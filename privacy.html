Donor Privacy & Data Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • S. A. Foundation (SAF)

At S. A. Foundation (SAF), registered as a charitable trust in India, we hold our donors, volunteers, and beneficiaries' trust with highest reverence. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is securely collected, handled, and safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a donation, register as a volunteer, or contact our team, we collect necessary identifying details, including:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Mailing Address.
  • Verification Details: Identification details required under statutory non-profit guidelines to issue valid donation receipts.
  • Transaction Records: Donation Amount, Date, UPI ID / Payment Method Reference (We do NOT store raw Credit/Debit card numbers or CVV codes; all processing happens via certified PCI-DSS payment gateways).

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized for the following non-commercial purposes:

  • Generating and issuing official Donation Receipts and accounting compliance.
  • Sending verified field execution updates and photographs from ground zero.
  • Coordinating volunteer assignments and background validation.
  • Sending quarterly transparency reports and annual audited financial summaries.

3. Zero Data-Selling Guarantee

S. A. Foundation NEVER sells, rents, leases, or trades donor or volunteer information to any third-party marketing companies, telemarketers, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

4. Data Security & Storage

All electronic transmission of donor information is encrypted using 256-bit SSL protocols. Records are stored in secure access-controlled databases complying with Indian IT Act provisions.

5. Your Rights & Opt-Out

You have the right to request access to your donation ledger, update your contact details, or unsubscribe from newsletter communications at any time by writing to contact@saf-foundation.org.
