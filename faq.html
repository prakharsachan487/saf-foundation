Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fund governance, digital tablet distribution, donations, and volunteer involvement.

Every project is monitored with GPS geo-tagged dispatch reporting and third-party audited financial balance sheets. Donors receive live photographic dispatches and updates from ground zero.

An official donation receipt with a unique transaction number and trust details is generated instantly upon completing your online or UPI donation. You can view, download, and print it right away, and a copy is emailed to your registered address.

Yes! You can donate directly by scanning our verified UPI QR Code on the home page or by sending contributions to our official UPI ID: safoundation@sbi. We also support Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and NetBanking through our secure payment gateway.

At least 88% of all received funds go directly into grassroots field execution (purchasing STEM science tablets, emergency flood relief dry rations, solar clean water pumps, and sewing craft machines). Only ~12% covers essential administrative, logistical, and auditing costs.

Our grassroots initiatives focus on underserved communities and flood-prone zones across Bihar (Patna, Kosi Basin, Madhubani, Gaya, Samastipur, Darbhanga, Nalanda) and adjoining rural districts.

You can fill out our interactive Volunteer Application Form. We offer both virtual opportunities (curriculum design, social media, translation) and field missions (teaching tablet classes, distributing relief packages, pediatric health camps). Certificates of service are provided upon program completion.

Yes, SAF Foundation is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for undertaking CSR activities (Form CSR-1). We partner with leading corporations to establish smart school tablet labs, solar water hubs, and women craft clusters with complete quarterly milestone audit reports.
