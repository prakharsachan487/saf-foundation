Our Areas of Work

Scalable grassroots interventions designed for measurable, generational impact.

Pillar 01 • Education

Quality Education & STEM

Digital smart classrooms, STEM lab tablets, and merit scholarships unlocking high school potential for rural children.

190+ Model Digital Schools
Pillar 02 • Nutrition

Food Security & Nutrition

Door-to-door distribution of fortified dry rations, infant supplementary meals, and community nutrition hubs.

1.2M+ Nutritious Meals Served
Pillar 03 • Healthcare

Primary Healthcare & Medicine

Mobile medical vans, free diagnostic health camps, essential generic medicines, and pediatric therapeutic care.

450k+ Patients Treated
Pillar 04 • Livelihood

Livelihoods & Skill Training

Vocational sewing hubs, solar craft production, digital job skills, and micro-enterprise incubation for rural youth.

12,000+ Youth & Women Skilled
Pillar 05 • Women

Women Self-Reliance & Farms

Establishing women-run cooperative farms, solar drinking water pumps, self-help microcredits, and leadership training.

320+ Self-Help Cooperatives

Disaster Relief & Rehabilitation

Rapid deployment of emergency flood rescue teams, clean drinking water packs, hygiene kits, and shelter rebuilds.

50,000+ Relief Kits Dispatched
